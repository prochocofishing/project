When to use this model +

When to use “cavala japonesa”?

During the day, especially with a high sun and clear water: the golden body reflects light, imitating a school of sardines or mackerel near the surface.

It is a very effective pattern in clear water, where the solid gold stands out in contrast to the intense sunlight.

The dot next to the hooks, resembling an eye, acts as a visual attraction point. By drawing on the cuttlefish’s natural tendency to attack prey around the head, it helps direct the strike towards the hooks.

Discover our story +

Great stories begin with a simple idea... ours was no different. It started with a deep passion for the sea and the craft of fishing. We believed, with unwavering conviction, that we could turn that love into something greater: creating high-quality fishing gear built to withstand hard use, serve those who live the sea and above all be truly effective. Armed with know‑how, the best materials and relentless dedication, we took on the challenge. What began as a simple passion became a mission, and a great story that keeps growing.
